## Media replacement notes

- Use six distinct, rights-cleared creator photos or video stills. Current Picsum images are random sample imagery, not real creators.
- Video is cropped with object-fit: cover; keep faces and important text inside the center safe area. A 9:16 video is recommended.
- Every video has a poster from the same image catalog. Videos load only when near the viewport and first previewed. They pause when out of view.
- Creator handles, performance figures and testimonial stories are illustrative. Replace them with verified, consented content before launch.
- Brand 01–08 are intentionally neutral local SVG wordmarks. No real brand logos are used.
- The logo mark, dotted map, country indicators, particle field, shader geometry and film grain are generated in code. No source image is needed for those.
- The Open Graph image is generated at /opengraph-image. To use a final static image, set assets.og.url to /assets/images/og.png.
- Add licensed Fontshare font files manually if their download API is unavailable. Use the exact three filenames above.
